Quotex floating draggable helper — Android Chrome bookmarklet
==============================================================

This bookmarklet adds a movable Q99 bubble on the CURRENT Quotex page. Drag the bubble with your finger to any position on the page; tap it to open/close the small panel. It does not read protected candle data or place trades.

INSTALL
1. Open any page in Chrome and bookmark it.
2. Edit that bookmark; name it "Q99 চার্ট সহকারী".
3. Replace the bookmark URL/address with the COMPLETE single line below. Save.
4. Open https://market-qx.info/en/trade and wait for the chart to load.
5. In Chrome's address bar, type the bookmark name "Q99 চার্ট সহকারী" and tap the matching bookmark suggestion (do not press Enter on a normal search).
6. The blue Q99 bubble should appear. Press and drag it anywhere. Tap it to open the panel.

LIMITS
- The bubble can move anywhere inside the current webpage viewport; it cannot float over other tabs or other apps.
- Some Chrome versions may block javascript: bookmark URLs. If the bookmark does nothing, Chrome may have stripped the javascript: prefix or blocked bookmarklet execution.
- A chart-like canvas/SVG being present does NOT mean its candle data can be read.
- No CALL/PUT prediction, guaranteed accuracy, or automated order placement is included.
