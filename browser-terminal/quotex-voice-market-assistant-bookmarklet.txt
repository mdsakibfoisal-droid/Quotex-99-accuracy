Quotex Voice Market Assistant — Android Chrome bookmarklet (demo)

WHAT IT DOES
- Floating draggable button and Bengali voice input/output where supported.
- Answers questions about whether chart-like elements are visible and reports how many visible chart-like DOM elements it found.
- Can explain candle concepts and give educational descriptions.
- IMPORTANT: finding canvas/SVG elements is NOT the same as reading candles. This bookmarklet does not access Quotex's private chart data, cannot reliably count candles or determine live candle direction, does not provide live trading signals, and does not place trades. It will say when it cannot verify chart data.
- Speech recognition may require internet, microphone permission, and compatible Chrome/device support. It is not connected to an AI service.

INSTALL
1. In Chrome, create a bookmark called Quotex Voice Assistant.
2. Edit the bookmark and replace its URL with the complete one-line javascript: URL below.
3. Open the Quotex chart page, then activate the bookmark from Bookmarks.
4. If Chrome blocks bookmarklets on that page, use a supported browser/hosted page approach instead. Never enter broker credentials into an untrusted panel.
